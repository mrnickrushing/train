Privacy Policy

Ironhorse Frontier · Rushing Technologies · Last updated November 3, 2026

Short version: Ironhorse Frontier doesn't require an account, doesn't show ads, doesn't track you across apps, and doesn't sell any data.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (your railroad, trains, town and settings) is saved only on your device. We don't receive it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on your devices. For this, RevenueCat receives an app user ID and your purchase history (which products you bought and when). The app user ID is a random identifier made on your device, or, if you sign in with Apple, the anonymous identifier Apple gives this app for your account. It's used only to deliver and restore what you bought. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

You can play without signing in. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the game an anonymous identifier for your account and, if you allow it, your first name. Your first name stays on your device and is only used to greet you in the game. We don't ask for your email address. The identifier is used as your RevenueCat app user ID (above) so your purchases follow you to your other devices.

Game Center

Achievements and leaderboards use Apple's Game Center and are covered by Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your Game Center data.

What we don't do

We don't collect your email, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier, and we don't use analytics or advertising SDKs that track you.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes your saved game. To remove purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us using the link below.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: Ironhorse Frontier support.

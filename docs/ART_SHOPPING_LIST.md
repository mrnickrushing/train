# Art shopping list

Three budget tiers for replacing the procedural art. All links are Unity Asset Store listings found
in November 2026. **Before buying, check each store page for current price, Unity 6 + URP
compatibility, polygon counts and license**; prices and versions change.

> **Style decision first.** The reference game uses a *semi-realistic* look. The cheapest packs
> below are *stylized low-poly*, which is clean, readable and mobile-friendly, but more toy-like.
> Pick one direction and buy packs that match it. Mixing styles is the most common way indie games
> end up looking cheap.

## Tier 2: $500–$2,000 (Tier 1 + a few custom hero trains)

Locomotives are what players collect and pay for, so they're where custom art pays off.

- Everything in Tier 1.
- **Commission 4–6 hero locomotives** from a 3D artist (ArtStation, Upwork, Fiverr Pro), roughly
  $150–$400 each depending on detail. Start with the starter engine, the Iron Bison, the
  Silver Arrow and the Thunderhead Twin, since those appear in offers and the season pass.
  Give the artist `docs/ART_PIPELINE.md` and the locomotive colors from `GameDatabase.cs`.
- Optional semi-realistic upgrade for the station area:
  [Industrial Train Station / Train Yard](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/industrial/industrial-train-station-train-yard-1934)
  (VIS Games, ~$50, very large download, so use selectively) or
  [Railway Pack](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/industrial/railway-pack-144704) (~$50).
- Custom UI icon set from an illustrator (~$200–500).

## Tier 3: $2,000+ (a unique look)

- An art director or lead artist to define the style (1–2 weeks).
- 15+ custom locomotives plus liveries for offers and pass rewards.
- Custom key buildings: depot, roundhouse, hotel, factories.
- A custom UI kit and character art for Hank the foreman.

## Other packs found (not recommended first)

- [Wild West Town - Realistic Buildings Pack](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/historic/wild-west-town-realistic-buildings-pack-338964): semi-realistic, but a large download (~415 MB).
- [Wild West Texas - Stylized Town Environment](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/wild-west-texas-stylized-town-environment-245331): stylized, ~216 MB.
- [Steam Trains and Stations (Low Poly)](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/historic/steam-trains-and-stations-low-poly-180116): trains and stations together.
- Final Form Studio "Locomotive" packs are detailed but very heavy (275 MB+ each) for mobile.

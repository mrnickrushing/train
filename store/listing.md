## Description

Ride the iron horse into the Old West.

Ironhorse Frontier puts you in charge of a young railroad in a wild, beautiful valley. Send steam locomotives out to the mines, ranches, farms and logging camps, haul their goods home, and turn a dusty railhead into a booming frontier town.

RUN A REAL RAILROAD
• Dispatch your trains to outposts across the territory and watch them steam through canyons, forests and over trestles
• Couple the right wagons for every job: flatcars for timber, hoppers for coal and ore, stock cars for cattle, boxcars for grain and mail
• Collect eight hand-crafted locomotives, from the plucky Dusty 0-4-0 switcher to mighty 2-8-2 freight haulers and gleaming streamliners
• Upgrade engines in the workshop so they pull more and run faster

BUILD A FRONTIER TOWN
• Fill orders from the townsfolk on the contract board for coins, XP and rare parts
• Build sawmills, smelters and workshops that turn raw goods into planks, steel and machine parts
• Watch your town grow from dirt tracks and log cabins to gravel streets, boardwalks and gas lamps as the years roll by

A STORY ACROSS THE TERRITORY
• Follow Hank the foreman, Clara the engineer and the ambitious Mr. Pemberton through seven chapters of the railroad's history
• String a telegraph line across the badlands, bridge the Crooked River gorge and race your rival to the golden spike
• Day turns to night: lamps glow along the platforms and headlights cut through the dark

Relaxed, satisfying and beautiful to look at, Ironhorse Frontier is a railroad tycoon you can play a few minutes at a time or all evening.

Ironhorse Frontier is free to play. Optional in-app purchases are available.

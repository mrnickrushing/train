Privacy Policy

Ironhorse Frontier · Rushing Technologies · Last updated November 3, 2026

Short version: Ironhorse Frontier doesn't ask for an account, doesn't show third-party ads, doesn't track you across apps, and doesn't sell any data.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (your railroad, trains, town and settings) is saved only on your device. We don't receive it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on your devices. For this, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device and your purchase history (which products you bought and when). It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID, and it's used only to deliver what you bought. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier, and we don't use analytics or advertising SDKs that track you.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes your saved game. To remove purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us using the link below.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: Ironhorse Frontier support.

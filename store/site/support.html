Restoring purchases

Open the Shop in the game and tap Restore Purchases. One-time purchases (like the Starter Pack and the Big Tender upgrade) come back on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your saved game

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
